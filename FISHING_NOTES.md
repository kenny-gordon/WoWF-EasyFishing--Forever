Last checked: 2026-10-02

This is a living reference for fishing-related content documented for WoW Forever.

## Source Status

The current evidence is the Wowhead Forever Fishing guide and the Forever database pages linked from it. Wowhead is a secondary reference, not the game publisher. The guide is labeled Patch 1.60.1, credited to Nodge, and marked updated 2026-09-29. It warns that WoW Forever is in beta and its content may be incomplete or change before launch.

No Blizzard-authored WoW Forever fishing patch note was verified during this research pass. Treat the facts below as documented Wowhead findings, not independently confirmed publisher statements. Recheck the guide and its changelog as the beta changes.

## Fishing in Forever

- Fishing is a secondary profession and does not use a primary-profession slot.
- The guide says fishing itself has not changed much. Forever-specific fishing content is mainly tied to cooking changes, camp crafting, and the Stranglethorn Vale Fishing Tournament.
- Fish are used in cooking recipes, including recipes for stat-increasing foods. The guide names Baked Salmon, Poached Sunscale Salmon, and Plated Armorfish as examples.
- Many fish-based cooking recipes have been added or buffed. Most cooked foods now grant 5% additional experience from kills.
- Fishing skill also unlocks three camp objects that can be placed at campfires: Fish Bowl, Fishing Rack, and Fishing Hut.
- A fishing pole is the only tool required. Basic poles are sold by Trade Supplies and Fishing Supplies vendors. Equip a pole, cast Fishing while facing water, then right-click the bobber when it splashes.
- The guide identifies no race with a direct Fishing racial bonus. It calls out Shaman utility from Far Sight and Water Walking for locating and reaching schools, and notes Hunters can feed many raw fish to pets.

## Artisan Quest

At character level 35 and Fishing 225, start Nat Pagle, Angler Extreme by speaking with Nat Pagle in Dustwallow Marsh at 58.6, 60.0. The quest requires four distinct fish that the guide says can only be caught while on the quest; they are not in special pools, and repeated casts may be needed:

- Feralas Ahi: Feralas, southeast of Dire Maul, around 63.5, 53.6.
- Misty Reed Mahi Mahi.
- Sar'theris Striker.
- Savage Coast Blue Sailfin.

The guide provides separate map tabs for the other three fish; their exact map points were not captured in this pass, so no coordinates are recorded here.

## Leveling

The guide says skill-ups depend on fish caught, not the difficulty of the fishing area.

| Fishing skill | Suggested approach |
|---:|---|
| 1-75 | Fish in any starter zone. Use Shiny Bauble below skill 25; at 25, starter zones have a 100% catch rate without the lure. |
| 75-150 | Learn Journeyman and fish in a capital city. The guide says these areas reach a 100% catch rate at 75 without lures. |
| 150-225 | Fish in Dustwallow Marsh or Stranglethorn Vale. The guide says 225 effective Fishing is needed for a 100% catch rate there; use boosts as needed. |
| 225-300 | The guide marks this section as coming soon; it does not provide a complete zone route. |

The guide suggests Stranglethorn Vale as generally more convenient for Horde and Dustwallow Marsh for Alliance. It also notes players can stay in a capital city if they are not focused on making money while leveling.

## Stranglethorn Vale Fishing Tournament

The guide documents two Forever changes to Master Angler:

- The first 50 players to complete the quest win, rather than only the first player.
- The quest always rewards an Arcanite Fishing Pole and an Extravagant Extravaganza Coin.

The coin can be exchanged for one of these items: Hook of the Master Angler, Surfer Shoes, Master Angler's Fishing Hat, Empty Fish Bucket, or Bitter Baitling.

## New Fishing Recipes

The guide lists these Fishing recipes for camp objects:

| Recipe | Minimum skill to craft | Recipe source and reagents |
|---|---:|---|
| Fish Bowl | 20 | Trainer; Raw Brilliant Smallfish + Empty Vial |
| Fishing Rack | 140 | Blueprint: Fishing Rack |
| Fishing Hut | 300 | Blueprint: Fishing Hut |

These objects can be placed at campsites. The Wowhead camping guide documents their Fishing benefits as follows:

- Fish Bowl: requires Fishing 20 to craft and to use. It needs a nearby campfire and gives nearby players 8% increased stats, which does not stack with Blessing of Kings. It shares a 1-hour cooldown with all campsite features.
- Fishing Rack: requires Fishing 140 to craft and use; its blueprint is the recipe source. It provides Fishing-skill lures, allows uncommon fish catches for 1 hour, and includes the Fish Bowl benefit. It needs a nearby campfire and can replace a Fish Bowl; campsite features share a 1-hour cooldown.
- Fishing Hut: requires Fishing 300 to craft and use; its blueprint is the recipe source. It provides Fishing-skill lures, allows rare fish catches for 1 hour, and includes the Fish Bowl benefit. It needs a nearby campfire and can replace a Fish Bowl; campsite features share a 1-hour cooldown.

Camping is described as a new crafting system alongside professions. The guide says a character receives The Great Outdoors around level 5, learns to craft a Basic Campfire through Cooking, and receives profession 101 quests from trained professions. A Basic Campfire supports three placed items, though a player can place only one item themselves at this stage.
